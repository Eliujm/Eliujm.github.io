ReActiva — Recommendation System

A category-level recommendation engine designed to generate personalized product suggestions from customer purchase behavior, deployed as a monitored cloud workload.

  • Built an XGBoost classifier on customer × category purchase cross-tabs, benchmarked against a popularity baseline via 3-fold cross-validation.
  • EDA showed the popularity baseline surfaced only 1 high-cost item in its top-5 picks, risking stagnant inventory — the classifier achieved far higher long-tail recall (0.18 vs. 0.00) instead.
  • Exposed recommendations through a Streamlit application and containerized the workload with Docker.
  • Deployed on AWS using S3, ECR, EC2, and EventBridge, with automated data-drift monitoring via Evidently.

Customer Payment-Default Prediction

An investigation into whether historical customer behavior carries enough signal to predict late payments — and an honest look at what happens when it doesn't.

  • Trained and compared four classifiers (GradientBoosting, RandomForest, Ridge, LDA) on a severely imbalanced dataset (4.7% late payments).
  • Used Optuna for hyperparameter optimization and MLflow to track experiments, parameters, and results.
  • Diagnosed that ~95% headline accuracy masked near-zero recall on late-paying customers, and tested class weighting, SMOTE, and threshold tuning to address it.
  • Finding: no threshold pushed precision and recall above ~20–25% together — the available features didn't carry enough signal, pointing to a data gap rather than a modeling one.
